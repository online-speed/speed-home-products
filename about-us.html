the secret to Speed Home Products’ success

Speed Home Products was founded in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, with a simple goal: manufacture cleaning products people could genuinely trust, batch after batch. Led by Managing Director Subbarao Polepalli — known across the trade as Speed Subbu — since its founding in 2003, the company has grown from a focused acid and phenyle range into a full catalogue spanning phenyles, toilet and bathroom cleaners, acids, disinfectant powders and cleaning tools, all manufactured in-house under ISO 9001:2008 certified processes. Today, Speed Home Products serves 500+ retail partners and distributors across Andhra Pradesh, with every bottle carrying Speed Subbu's personal commitment to quality — nothing shortcut, nothing compromised.
