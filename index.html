About Us

A Trusted Name in Home Cleaning, Since 2003

Speed Home Products was founded in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, with a simple goal: manufacture cleaning products people could genuinely trust, batch after batch. What began with a focused acid and phenyle range has grown, over 23 years, into a full catalogue spanning phenyles, toilet and bathroom cleaners, acids, disinfectant powders and cleaning tools — all manufactured in-house at our own facility.
